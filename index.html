I am an Assistant Professor in Game Theory at the Department of Advanced Computing Sciences, Maastricht University, where I also coordinate the Research Area Games.

My research focuses on bridging mathematical models and clinical applications, with a particular interest in modeling cancer treatment through evolutionary game theory.
