Research

My research lies at the intersection of game theory, mathematical modeling, and clinical applications. I am particularly interested in how evolutionary game theory can help us understand and model cancer treatment.

research interests

  • Evolutionary game theory
  • Mathematical oncology
  • Modeling cancer therapy
  • Translating mathematical models to clinical settings
